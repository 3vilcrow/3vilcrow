I designed and built the **TwoFaces mobile app in Flutter** for iOS and Android: sellers scan a book's barcode, chat with the same AI agent that runs on WhatsApp, send photos and voice notes, and follow their orders, all in one place. It shares the backend, orders and history with the WhatsApp channel, using the phone number as the shared identity.

**Built with** Flutter · BLoC · go_router · Dio · barcode scanning · offline storage with SQLite · Firebase Analytics · Sentry · Fastlane for store releases.

### 🚀 What I'm building

As **CTO of TwoFaces** I lead the whole technical side of a second-hand book marketplace, from the cloud architecture to the apps the team uses every day:

- 🤖 **Conversational AI agent on WhatsApp** that talks to sellers, reads photos of books, prices them in real time and closes orders end to end.
- 📚 **Pricing and marketplace engine** connected to the Amazon Selling Partner API, with margin rules, sales-rank signals and inventory analytics.
- 📦 **Logistics automation**: shipping labels, carrier integrations and push tracking with automatic WhatsApp notifications.
- 💶 **Payments and finance**: automated SEPA payouts to sellers and bank reconciliation.
- 📱 **TwoFaces app for iOS and Android** (see above), plus the public website built with Astro.
- 📊 **Back office in Flutter** with real-time metrics, conversion dashboards and prompt versioning for the bot.
- ☁️ **Infrastructure as code**: I led the migration from SAM to **AWS CDK**, with separate dev and prod environments.
